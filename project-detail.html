Ming Motor | Website & System ERP

Supply Chain & Inventory Automation

For Efficiency and Human Error Reduction

Overview

Ming Motor is a prominent automotive spare parts retailer. They manage a high-volume daily inventory to support both wholesale and retail sales, as well as integrated motorcycle maintenance services.

Challenges

Ming Motor faced significant operational hurdles, including inventory shrinkage, inconsistent data, and a manual pricing system that relied on staff memory. This manual-heavy workflow led to frequent sales errors and increased the risk of operational losses.

Solutions

We designed a web-based Supply Chain Management (SCM) system that integrates the entire goods flow in real-time. Featuring an Automated Report Dashboard and Barcode Scanner Integration, this system minimizes human errors, ensures 100% stock data accuracy, and accelerates in-store transaction processes.
